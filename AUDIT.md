## 3. Oracle (`erp.py`) behaviour — as read and as tested

gross = Σ line_base − header_discount + Σ line_taxes + Σ header_taxes + freight + insurance + extra + excise, rounded half-up.

- Line base: `qty × unit_price`; `discount_percentage` wins over `discount`; amount discount is a **total** for the line.
- A tax with a non-empty `tax_amount` uses it; otherwise it is derived from `tax_rate` (line taxes on the line base,
  header taxes on `Σ line_base − header_discount`). Charges are never in the header-tax base.
- The header discount is **not** apportioned to line-level taxes.
- Negative `tax_amount` reduces the gross (withholding).
- `num()` **silently returns 0.0** for `1,234.56`, `73,00`, `73.00 EUR`, `RM100.00`, `S$100`, `R100`. Only leading `€ $ £ ¥ ₹` and spaces are stripped. Serialisation must therefore always emit plain dot-decimals.
- No sign handling exists; a `CREDIT_MEMO` with positive values books a positive gross.
All of these are pinned by tests in `tests/test_baseline.py` (37 tests, all PASS).
